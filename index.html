About Us

A Spanish gastronomical experience.

Pascual is a place you can celebrate life with friends and family, with the company of tapas, cañas and special drinks.

We believe that food always brings us together, takes us to special places and creates memories that will stay with us forever.

Pascual is the uncle of a Spanish family; he was most importantly the loyal dog of our founder's grandfather. Our dog is where our home is. We can always find happiness arriving to our loyal and happy friend that knows and always smiles for us.

We want to create a relaxed, professional and unique experience with tapas and a good wine. We recommend starting with two tapas per person and continuing with a paella or main course.

We are located inside the beautiful town of Las Catalinas, beachfront to the free salty air of Guanacaste.
